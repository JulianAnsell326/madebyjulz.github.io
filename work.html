How I work.

I write your brief back to you.

Before I shoot, you get one page: who it's for, the single thing it has to say, and three hook options. You pick, then I film. No surprises in the edit.

Built for the first second.

Every video opens on the product doing something, not on me saying hello. I deliver hook variations so you can test which opening holds.

Real use, not a backdrop.

Products get used on an ordinary day in Sydney: on the walk, at the bench, on the couch. Pet briefs come with a co-star, Molly, a golden retriever who has opinions about food.

Clean files.

No watermark, no handle, no creator branding on your deliverables unless you want it. Raw clips included so your team can recut.
